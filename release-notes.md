## Draft 0.5.0

A calmer, smoother Draft, with a History tab and a real choice of model.

- **History tab.** See your newest dictations with their age and length. Copy or delete any of them, or delete everything with a two-click confirmation. History is still text and duration only, on this PC, and old entries expire automatically.
- **Choose your model.** Settings > Recognition is now a seven-step ladder in plain words (Most accurate, Accurate, Light and so on) with the size, who each suits, whether it is already on your PC, and a "Recommended for this PC" marker. New: **Small English** and **Base English**, which are built for English only and are usually a little cleaner in English at the same size.
- **Smoother everywhere.** Screens fade in when you switch tabs or sections, hover and press ease instead of snapping, and a color blend that made every hover flash muddy is fixed. Nothing animates while Draft sits idle, and it follows Windows' reduced-motion setting.
- **A useful first screen.** When you are not speaking, Dictate shows your three most recent dictations instead of an empty meter. The meter appears only while you are recording.
- **Honest numbers.** The idle meter used to print a fixed "LAST 1.6 s". It is gone.

Free and MIT licensed. Windows 10/11 x64.

**Not code-signed yet**, so Windows SmartScreen may warn: choose More info, then Run anyway. Verify the SHA-256 files first. The standard installer (11.4 MB) downloads your model once on first launch; `Draft-Setup-0.5.0-offline.exe` (1.43 GB) bundles the large model and needs no download.
